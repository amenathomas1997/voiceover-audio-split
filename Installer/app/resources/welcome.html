VoiceOver Audio Split

VoiceOver Audio Split puts VoiceOver's speech in one ear and every other sound on your Mac in the other, like the audio split in JAWS. It also adds tools for sound, reading, the camera, documents, braille, typing practice and more, all from the keyboard. Headphones work best.

The app is installed in your Applications folder. Your Mac asks for your password, as it does for any app installed there.

If the older script version of VoiceOver Audio Split is installed, it is removed, because its keyboard shortcuts clash with the app's.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later.
