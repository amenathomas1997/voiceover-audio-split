Two quick steps to finish

1. Allow the keyboard shortcuts

System Settings has opened on Privacy and Security, Accessibility. Find VO Audio Split Keys in the list and turn it on. If it isn't listed, press Right Option and Left Arrow once, then look again.

2. Allow the sound split

The first time you press Right Option and Left Arrow, your Mac asks whether VO Audio Split may record system audio. Choose Allow. The app needs this to move sounds between your ears. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Optional: hear announcements in VoiceOver's voice

Open VoiceOver Utility with VoiceOver key and F8. In General, check "Allow VoiceOver to be controlled with AppleScript". If you skip this, announcements use the Mac's standard voice.

More options and uninstalling

More commands are in your Applications folder, inside the folder called VoiceOver Audio Split, under Scripts. They include starting the split when you log in and resetting the volume balance. The same folder has Uninstall VoiceOver Audio Split, which removes everything.
