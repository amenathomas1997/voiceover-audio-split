VoiceOver Audio Split is installed

It's in your Applications folder, and it has opened. It runs in the menu bar, with no Dock icon.

The setup guide walks you through the permissions it needs: the keyboard shortcuts, the sound split, and announcements in VoiceOver's voice.

Once it's set up, hold the Option key on the right side of the keyboard and press M to open the app's menu, with every command and Settings. Right Option and Left Arrow puts VoiceOver in your left ear.

To open it again later, find VoiceOver Audio Split in Applications, Spotlight or Launchpad.
