VoiceOver Audio Split

This puts VoiceOver's voice in one ear and every other sound on your Mac in the other ear, like the audio split feature in JAWS. Headphones work best.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Right Option and Left Arrow: speech in the left ear. Press again to turn the split off.
  • Right Option and Right Arrow: speech in the right ear. Press again to turn the split off.
  • Right Option and Up Arrow: speech louder.
  • Right Option and Down Arrow: other sounds louder.

Use the Option key on the right side of the keyboard. The left Option key keeps working as usual.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later. No administrator password is needed. Everything is installed in your own user account.
